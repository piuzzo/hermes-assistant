Hermes Assistant

A private, single-user personal assistant running on a home Raspberry Pi.

Hermes Assistant is a personal instance of the open-source Hermes Agent, self-hosted by its owner on a Raspberry Pi. It is not a public service and is used by its owner only.

Use of Google data

The app uses the Google Calendar API, with the owner's consent, to read the owner's calendar events and to create or delete events on request. It does not access any other Google service.

Italiano. Hermes Assistant è un assistente personale privato, ospitato dal proprietario su un Raspberry Pi e usato solo dal proprietario stesso. Usa Google Calendar, con il suo consenso, per leggere, creare ed eliminare eventi del suo calendario.
